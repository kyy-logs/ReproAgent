assertion_sites.execution_observation is a program observation, not a semantic approval or coverage report. failure_location_overlap means a checked failed phase has a unique candidate frame/crash line-range overlap; this does not prove assertion entry (a preceding statement can fail on the same line), an exception evaluating an assertion operand can produce this too, so it does not prove that its comparison ran. phase_indexes indexes observation.tests (whose full details may be in failure_evidence). not_established means no such proof was recorded: do not infer either pass or skip, including for assertions in passing tests. Review every later/unobserved assertion independently; two repeats of an earlier failure do not exercise those later checks.

literal_comparison checks expose the exact strings and operators imposed by ordinary asserts, including simple literal bindings and concatenations. For EACH, explain in reason why that precision is necessary for the reported behavior or a source-backed control in this input/builder/configuration. A source-backed example may support a reference's meaning without guaranteeing byte-for-byte escaping, markup, label serialization or formatting in another scenario. Assess the expression as a whole, not just a recognizable word. Existing-semantics support is legitimate when the supplied source establishes the actual mapping in this scenario; do not reject precise output explicitly required by the report or an established API. If the exact constraint is incidental, unsupported or unresolved, set supported=false even when a primary failure is genuine. These checks are questions, never automatic defects. No fixed-version result or gold evidence may justify removing or accepting an assertion.
